THE MANIFESTO

Why Ethical, Local-First AI Matters

The mainstream AI industry has defaulted to cloud centralization, perpetual monthly rents, and opaque data pipelines. VIGAM Labs was founded on the opposite premise.

01 / ARCHITECTURE

Data Sovereignty

Proprietary unreleased stems, confidential engineering reports, or trade secrets must not be streamed to third-party cloud servers for inference. Processing on-device ensures your IP remains yours.

02 / ECONOMICS

Perpetual Ownership

Software you rely on daily should not cease working if an API provider raises prices or goes bankrupt. We reject predatory SaaS models: pay once, enjoy lifetime utility with 1.x updates.

03 / PERFORMANCE

Sub-Millisecond Edge

Eliminating network round-trips removes latency jitter and server downtime. Optimized C++, Rust, and compact neural models deliver lightning-fast responses directly through your CPU cache.

04 / ETHICS

Zero Dark Patterns

No mandatory email logins just to search your own files. No hidden background telemetries pinging foreign servers. Clean, quiet, deterministic tools that respect user autonomy.

Flagship Project: KLANG

Our inaugural production system: a high-speed, local neural audio search engine built for music producers, sound designers, and audio engineers.

PRODUCTION RELEASE · v1.0 BETA
Platform: macOS & Windows 10/11 Compute: 100% Local CPU Model: Multi-Modal Audio-Text Transformer

KLANG /klæŋ/

"Search your samples by sound, not filename."

kick_04_raw_final.wav tells you nothing. Most sample managers only search filenames and folder names. KLANG listens to the audio itself—finding the exact sound you have in mind across tens of thousands of local samples in sub-15ms, completely offline without a dedicated GPU.

KLANG Desktop App · Sub-15ms semantic matching across local sample library

⚡

Natural Language Audio Search

Type "dusty lo-fi snare" or "warm tape 808". Results update live, ranked by acoustic neural similarity rather than folder tags.

🎙️

On-Device Voice-to-Sound

Click the mic, speak or beatbox your sound idea. Audio is transcribed completely locally on CPU and matches instant results without hitting remote APIs.

🎯

"Find Similar" Reference Matching

Drop an existing one-shot, loop, or reference track. KLANG embeds the acoustic features and re-ranks your entire library by cosine distance.

🎛️

Direct DSP Acoustic Filtering

Filter by physical audio attributes: attack sharpness, tonal warmth, and length—computed mathematically from raw waveforms.

🎹

Synth Preset Intelligence

Deep neural indexing for Vital, Surge XT, and u-he presets. Inspect patch envelopes, filters, and effects without opening your synth.

🎚️

Seamless DAW Drag-and-Drop

Drag results directly into Ableton Live, FL Studio, Logic Pro, Reaper, Cubase, or Bitwig. No file copying, no intermediate format conversion.
